# NEXA INTELLITECH &bull; Commercial Automation Services Website

> A public-facing commercial marketing & service website for **Nexa Intellitech Solutions**, built with a crisp **Professional Light Theme**, **Tailwind CSS**, dynamic **PHP**, and interactive client experience widgets.

---

## 🌟 What This Public Website Delivers

1. **Public Marketing Homepage (`/` or `index.php`)**:
   - High-converting hero section with enterprise credentials and trust statistics (1,450+ Homes, 420+ Offices, 120+ SCADA Plants, 34% Average Energy Reduction).
   - **Interactive Experience Center Widget**: Right on the landing page, clients can test drive the **3D Motorized Venetian Blinds Slat Tilt** (-90° to +90°), **PDLC Smart Glass Dynamic Opacity Slider** (0% to 100%), and **AI Building Agent Voice Assistant**.
   - **Services Showcase Cards**: Highlighting Home Automation, Office Facilities, Industrial Factory SCADA, Architectural Smart Windows, and AI Agents.
   - **Instant Turnkey Cost Estimator Widget**: Prospective clients can select their property type, slide their square footage (800 to 25,000+ sq ft), pick an AI tier, and see immediate budget estimates.
   - **Why Choose Us Matrix**: Licensed master electrical contractors, open-bus architecture (KNX, Matter, BACnet, Modbus, Lutron), and 24/7 SLA monitoring.
   - **Case Studies & Portfolios**: Verified projects in Aspen (Villa), Austin (Corporate HQ), and Detroit (Automotive Assembly).
   - **Client Testimonials**: 5-star customer reviews.
   - **Consultation & Quote Request Form**: Working submission connected to `api/quote.php`.

2. **Dedicated Services Catalog (`index.php?page=services`)**:
   - In-depth specifications, protocols supported, and equipment brands for Residential Smart Homes, Corporate Office Automation, Factory SCADA Robotics, and Architectural Glazing.

3. **Specialized Smart Window & PDLC Glass Page (`index.php?page=smart-glass`)**:
   - Interactive 3D window physics visualizer with dual-axis sliders, SHGC ratings, and rooftop astronomical solar tracking sensors.

4. **Autonomous AI Agents Solutions (`index.php?page=ai-solutions`)**:
   - Breakdown of **Helios** (Energy/Microgrid), **Aura** (Circadian/Windows), **Aegis** (Security/Vision), and **Vulcan** (SCADA/Machinery).

5. **Projects Portfolio & Case Studies (`index.php?page=case-studies`)**:
   - Real-world ROI metrics, project scopes, and client testimonials.

6. **Transparent Turnkey Pricing (`index.php?page=pricing`)**:
   - 4 clear tiers: Villa Essential ($12.5k), Smart Glass ($18.9k), Office Enterprise ($28.5k), and Factory SCADA ($48k).

7. **About Company & Licenses (`index.php?page=about`)**:
   - Engineering credentials, ISO 9001 and SIL-3 safety certifications, and company philosophy.

8. **Contact & Assessment Booking (`index.php?page=contact`)**:
   - Free CAD blueprint consultation form, direct phone line, email, and showroom addresses.

9. **Interactive Tech Telemetry Console (`index.php?page=demo`)**:
   - Live client experience console where prospective clients can test drive the underlying real-time telemetry dashboard.
